# References

The Plant Pulse skill cites these sources in the generated report's "Sources & Benchmarks" section. Only sources listed here are used — if a benchmark or play has no authoritative source, the skill flags it honestly rather than inventing one.

---

1. **SMRP Best Practices, 5th Edition.** Society for Maintenance & Reliability Professionals, 2017 (with updates). The de facto North American standard for maintenance and reliability metrics. https://smrp.org

2. **Palmer, Doc.** *Maintenance Planning and Scheduling Handbook*, 3rd ed. McGraw-Hill, 2019. ISBN 978-1260135282. Canonical reference on planner/scheduler practice, wrench time, and backlog.

3. **Moore, Ron.** *Making Common Sense Common Practice: Models for Operational Excellence*, 4th ed. Reliabilityweb Press, 2017. ISBN 978-1941872390.

4. **Moubray, John.** *Reliability-Centered Maintenance (RCM II)*, 2nd ed. Industrial Press, 1997. ISBN 978-0831131463.

5. **Nowlan, F. Stanley & Heap, Howard F.** *Reliability-Centered Maintenance.* United Airlines / US Department of Defense, 1978. Public-domain foundational RCM study. https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/citations/ADA066579

6. **SAE JA1011:2009** — *Evaluation Criteria for Reliability-Centered Maintenance (RCM) Processes.* https://www.sae.org/standards/content/ja1011_200908/

7. **SAE JA1012:2011** — *A Guide to the Reliability-Centered Maintenance (RCM) Standard.* https://www.sae.org/standards/content/ja1012_201108/

8. **ISO 55000:2014 / ISO 55001:2014** — *Asset management.* https://www.iso.org/standard/55088.html

9. **ISO 14224:2016** — *Petroleum, petrochemical and natural gas industries — Collection and exchange of reliability and maintenance data for equipment.* https://www.iso.org/standard/64076.html

10. **Wireman, Terry.** *Benchmarking Best Practices in Maintenance Management*, 2nd ed. Industrial Press, 2004. Companion: *MRO Inventory and Purchasing* (2008).

11. **Nakajima, Seiichi.** *Introduction to TPM: Total Productive Maintenance.* Productivity Press, 1988. Origin of the 85% OEE "world-class" figure — cite with the Hansen caveat below.

12. **Hansen, Robert C.** *Overall Equipment Effectiveness: A Powerful Production/Maintenance Tool for Increased Profits.* Industrial Press, 2001. Critical counterweight to the universal-85% OEE claim.

13. **Gulati, Ramesh.** *Maintenance and Reliability Best Practices*, 2nd ed. Industrial Press, 2012. Aligned to SMRP Body of Knowledge.

14. **Latino, Robert J. et al.** *Root Cause Analysis: Improving Performance for Bottom-Line Results*, 4th ed. CRC Press, 2011.

15. **US Bureau of Labor Statistics — Injuries, Illnesses, and Fatalities (IIF) Program.** Annual. Authoritative source for NAICS-specific TRIR benchmarks. https://www.bls.gov/iif/
